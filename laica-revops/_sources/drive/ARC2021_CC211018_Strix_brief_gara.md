## TENDER MAIN GOAL
UX DESIGN PROCESS TO ECOMMERCE SUCCESS: BUILDING STRIX D2C DIGITAL CHANNELS
The group is looking to build its own digital D2C channels including eCommerce, Social Media, Applications and CRM tools. In order to identify the key requirements that will drive the development of the digital platform, Strix has decided to run a User Experience Design project with the aim to find out how to best meet consumers needs and differentiate its owned Brands from the digital competition.

WHY A USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN PROJECT?
- To find out what the users really need
- To solve their problems
- To provide positive experiences
- To keep a user loyal
- To define the most successful customer journeys on our products

HOW DO WE CONVINCE PEOPLE TO BUY OUR BRAND?
DIFFERENTIATION
- Strategic Market Analysis: which unsatisfied needs our products could address?
- Buyer persona: which are ideal consumers to attract?
- Define a Strategy to differentiate our Brands from competitors.
CLIENT GENERATION ENGINE
- Focus the Brand storytelling around consumers value (organic & paid)
- Define a Primer Offer / Incentive
- Build a Marketing Funnel(s) Approach
- Start a Lead Generation strategy and a nurturing program
PROFIT MAXIMIZATION
- Focus on CONVERSIONS, optimize consumer experience, A/B testing
- Develop a product offer to enable UpSell and CrossSell (Exclusives, Advanced, Collaborations)
- Activate scenarios and functionalities to make reorders easy
- High marginality

## STRATEGIC FRAME
STRIX MUST PLAY A KEY ROLE IN CONSUMERS JOURNEY TO GENERATE VALUE BY BUILDING AN INTEGRATED DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER STRATEGY.
Digital Platform - DIGITAL GOALS: Top of Mind; Customer Lifetime Value. WHAT: Brand Credibility, Brand Authority, Brand Identity; Re-Order, Purchase, Consideration to Buy. HOW: Paid Advertising, Lead Generation, Organic – SEO, Nurturing content, Newsletter, Retargeting. Digital Marketing; Marketplaces Vendor vs Seller.
DIGITAL PILLARS: 1. Differentiation; 2. Client Generation Engine (Content, Primer Offer, Copywriting, Cross Sell, Story telling); 3. Profit Maximization (Marginalization; Monitor, Defend, Adjust).

## D2C DIGITAL ENGINE - KEY PLAN OBJECTIVES: TARGETING CONSUMERS IN ALL STEPS OF THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY
KPIS: UPPER FUNNEL Awareness (Reach/Impressions, Sessions/Page views, Click Through Rate, Cost Per Click); Interest (#Pages per Session, Session time); MIDDLE FUNNEL Consideration / Intent (eCommerce); LOW FUNNEL Evaluation / Purchase (Add to Cart / Orders, Conversion Rate, Cost per Conversion, Average Order Value); POST PURCHASE Engagement (Returns, Additional Orders, Re-orders, Re-Purchase, Rates & Reviews).
ORGANIC (SEO strategy, Brand website, Search Engine, Blog, Social Media); PAID (ADV, Social Media, Re-targeting); OWNED D2C (Lead Generation, CRM / Newsletter, Social Media, App...). BE PRESENT & RELEVANT / ATTRACT NEW CONSUMERS / ENGAGE & CONVERT.

HOW DO WE MEASURE OUR GROWTH?
- TOP OF MIND: Reach / Impressions / Sessions / Page view; CTR (Click Through rate); CPC (Cost Per Click)
- CUSTOMER LIFETIME VALUE: Orders; Conversion rate; Cost per conversion; AOV (Average Order Value)

## THE TENDER BRIEF
BRIEF GOALS: Outline strategic steps and key activities in order to develop the most satisfying digital customer experience for Strix brands (Astrea, LAICA, Aqua Optima) within Water Filtration category.
CUSTOM NEEDS: We must know our audience to understand what are their problems and how they look for a solution. Starting from the already performed studies on personas Strix wants to identify specific digital opportunities to focus on within the water category, going beyond water filtration and including water solutions / offers. What Users really need; Problems identification; Digital cross-channels consumers behavior. The outcome is full understanding of our personas and their problems, actionable findings to effectively engage with them digitally.
VALUE PROPOSITION: Together with Strix Marketing team we have to structure the value propositions of our Brands Astrea, Aqua Optima, Laica and define their strategic positioning in the market. Digital competitors benchmark (beyond filtration); Key digital differentiation opportunities; Build a Strategic approach to cross-channels digital commerce. The outcome is define the Brand strategy to connect with, interact with and grow our customers base.
DEVELOPMENT: How to? We want to build a digital infrastructure to fully implement and execute our Brand Digital Strategy. The project will allow us to identify what we need and to define a tailor made scope of new Strix B2C Digital Platform development. UX design: cross-channels customer journeys; UX eCommerce: functionalities & requirements; Identification of Platform components; Guidelines for technology selection. The outcome is Precise Business and Technical requirements to scope out the Platform development project.
