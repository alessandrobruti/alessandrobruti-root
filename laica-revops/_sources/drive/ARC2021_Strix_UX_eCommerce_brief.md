## Genesis - Strix Digital Platform to enable B2C digital channels

### COMPANY BACKGROUND - Key facts
Strix Group is Innovating safety and design for a sustainable future.
Headquartered in the first UNESCO biosphere on the Isle of Man, the group develops award winning technologies that power leading brands to deliver un-rivaled safety and helps reducing energy consumption, single use plastics and water contamination around the world.
As a publicly listed company on the London stock exchange (AIM: KETL) Strix attracts a range of investors across the technology and sustainability sectors. The group has a global footprint with over 800 employees across its manufacturing facilities in China and Italy, its commercial offices in the UK and USA, and a sales team covering the globe.
The group's core business is the design and manufacture of Kettle Safety controls where it maintains a marketing leading position - controlling 54% of the global market share - and holds key relationships with leading brands and OEMs.
Through investments in key acquisitions and R&D, the group also innovates technologies in the Small Domestic Appliance and Water Filtration segments, where it brings its technologies to market through its own consumer brands and with key brand partners around the world.
The group is looking to build its own digital D2C channels including eCommerce, Social Media, Applications and CRM tools. In order to identify the key requirements that will drive the development of the digital platform, Strix has decided to run a User Experience Design project with the aim to find out how to best meet consumers needs and differentiate its owned Brands from the digital competition.

WHY A USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN PROJECT? Find out what the users really need; How to solve their problems; Provide positive experiences; Keep a user loyal; Define the most successful customer journeys on our products.

### BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
- CUSTOMERS NEEDS: We must know our audience to understand what are their problems and how they look for a solution. Starting from the already performed studies on personas Strix wants to identify specific digital opportunities to focus on within the water category, going beyond water filtration and including water solutions / offers. OUTCOME: Full understanding of our personas and their problems, actionable findings to effectively engage with them digitally.
- VALUE PROPOSITION: Together with Strix Marketing team we have to structure the value propositions of our Brands – Astrea, Aqua Optima, Laica – and define their strategic positioning in the market. This will guide us to execute relevant experiences according to our goals. OUTCOME: Define the Brand strategy to connect with, interact with and grow our customers base.
- DEVELOPMENT: How to? We want to build a digital infrastructure to fully implement and execute our Brand Digital Strategy. The project will allow us to identify what we need and to define a tailor made scope of new Strix B2C Digital Platform development. OUTCOME: Precise Business and Technical requirements to scope out the Platform development project.

## Strategic Frame - Strix Digital Platform to enable D2C digital channels
STRIX MUST PLAY A KEY ROLE IN CONSUMERS JOURNEY TO GENERATE VALUE BY BUILDING AN INTEGRATED DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER STRATEGY. Digital Platform / Digital Marketing / Marketplaces Vendor vs Seller. DIGITAL GOALS: Top of Mind (Brand Credibility, Brand Authority, Brand Identity; Paid Advertising, Organic – SEO); Customer Lifetime Value (Re-Order, Purchase, Consideration to Buy; Lead Generation, Nurturing content, Newsletter, Retargeting).
DIGITAL PILLARS - How do we convince people to buy our Brand?
1. Differentiation: Strategic Market Analysis: which unsatisfied needs our products could address?; Buyer persona: which are ideal consumers to attract?; Define a Strategy to differentiate our Brands from competitors.
2. Client Generation Engine: Focus the Brand storytelling around consumers value (organic & paid); Define a Primer Offer / Incentive; Build a Marketing Funnel(s) Approach; Start a Lead Generation strategy and a nurturing program.
3. Profit Maximization: Focus on CONVERSIONS, optimize consumer experience, A/B testing; Develop a product offer to enable UpSell and CrossSell (Exclusives, Advanced, Collaborations); Activate scenarios and functionalities to make reorders easy; High marginality.
D2C DIGITAL ENGINE (KPIs per upper/middle/low funnel e post purchase, come nel brief CC211018): Reach/Impressions, Sessions/Page views, CTR, CPC; #Pages per Session, Session time; Add to Cart / Orders, Conversion Rate, Cost per Conversion, AOV; Returns, Additional Orders, Re-orders, Rates & Reviews. ORGANIC / PAID / OWNED.
D2C DIGITAL ENGINE: MARKETING AUTOMATION: Traffic → Squeeze page → Email → Primer offer → Buy now → Cross sell / Up sell; Email nurturing 1, 2, X; Cold contact → Sales page → Remarketing → Buy now $$ → Hot contact; Referral.
DIGITAL GROWTH: Market penetration → Market expansion.
How do we measure our Growth? Top of Mind: Reach/impressions/Sessions/Page views, CTR, CPC. Customer Lifetime Value: Orders, Conversion rate, Cost per Conversion, AOV.

## The Brief - User Experience Design Project
BRIEF GOALS: Outline strategic steps and key activities in order to develop the most satisfying digital customer experience for Strix brands (Astrea, LAICA, Aqua Optima) within Water Filtration category.
CUSTOMER NEEDS: What Users really need; Problems identification; Digital cross-channels consumers behavior. VALUE PROPOSITION: Digital competitors benchmark (beyond filtration); Key digital differentiation opportunities; Build a Strategic approach to cross-channels digital commerce. DEVELOPMENT: UX design: cross-channels customer journeys; UX eCommerce: functionalities & requirements; Identification of Platform components; Guidelines for technology selection.
**REQUIRED TIMELINE: 3/4 MONTHS**
